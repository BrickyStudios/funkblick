An open-source alternative to SØPHIA

SØPHIA is a paid passive Wi-Fi and Bluetooth scanner sold on Gumroad. Funkblick does the same job for free, with the source on GitHub, and adds tracker protection, speed cameras and channel tips. Comparison based on SØPHIA's product page in November 2026.

Download the APKSource on GitHub

Free, Android 8.0 or newer, runs on GrapheneOS without Google services. No account. The app fetches its own updates from GitHub.
